# Screen inventory (visual enhancement, step 1)

**Date:** 2026-11-16
**Scope:** every route under `app/`, what it is for, what data it really has, and what it already does. Nothing here proposes a change; the proposals are in [`visualisation-placement.md`](visualisation-placement.md).

**How it was inspected:** each `page.tsx` and the screen component it renders, the `messages/en.json` namespace it reads, the API route behind it, and a signed-in tour of the local seeded demo (Fernhill Ceramics) on desktop and at 375 px.

## What every screen shares

| Thing                | Where                                                        | Note                                                                                                                                                |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Frame and navigation | `components/nav/AppFrame.tsx`                                | Ink rail, six groups, tab strip, Emergency Stop. `/report` and `/proposals` are focused views with their own masthead.                              |
| Tokens               | `app/sheet.css`, `specs/ux-spec-v1.md` §2                    | White paper sheets on the ink desk. Zilla Slab, Archivo, IBM Plex Mono. Green accent. Square corners, no shadows, no icons.                         |
| Page furniture       | `components/sheet/PageHead.tsx`                              | `PageHead` (kicker, Zilla headline) and `SheetCard` (`.cs-*`).                                                                                      |
| Tables               | `.rt`, `.rt-stack` in `app/sheet.css`                        | Ruled table; stacks under 720 px.                                                                                                                   |
| Disclosures          | `components/sheet/Fold.tsx`, `components/report/WhyFold.tsx` | Two near-identical components.                                                                                                                      |
| Figures              | `components/sheet/num.ts`                                    | `moneyText`, `moneyFig`, `num`: integer minor units plus currency, in the reader's locale.                                                          |
| Charts               | `components/charts/*`, `app/charts.css`                      | Plain SVG, no library. Sheet tokens only. Filled = up or measured, hollow = down. Every chart has a summary sentence and a hidden table. No cobalt. |
| Basis words          | `components/report/basis.ts`                                 | OBSERVED, DERIVED, STATED, ESTIMATED: every figure carries one.                                                                                     |
| Languages            | `messages/*.json`                                            | Seven locales. Every new string needs all seven.                                                                                                    |

## Data that does not exist (so no visual may pretend it does)

- **Ads:** impressions, click-through rate, cost per click, cost per thousand, frequency, and anything below campaign level. Clicks and conversions exist for Google only.
- **Organic content:** likes, comments, shares and saves as separate figures (one "interactions" total exists); followers; video retention curves.
- **Attribution:** no path data linking a customer to the channels they came through.
- **Cohorts:** no cohort retention table; lifecycle counts exist instead.
- **Profit over time:** revenue is measured daily; profit and expenses are derived once per report, not as a series.
- **Competitors:** no spend or reach figures.
