## Idea 54: its own track record (owner, 2026-10-29: "go to the next one")

What it doesn't grade is SYB's **forecasts**: the cash range, the stock-out dates, the busy seasons and the pipeline's likely close. Idea 54 records each forecast at the moment it is shown, with its range, and grades it once its window has passed, against measured data only. Rule 25 holds: every forecast states its basis. Two choices are made here, conservatively:

- **Nothing is recorded after the fact.** A forecast enters the record only when it was made, with the range shown then. ## Idea 44: compared with similar businesses (owner, 2026-10-29)

The owner sees a few of their own numbers set against businesses like theirs: same activity, same country, same size. The source is Eurostat's structural business statistics, the dataset `sbs_sc_ovw` ("Enterprise statistics by size class and NACE Rev. 2 activity, from 2021 onwards"). It is free and keyless, reusable under CC BY 4.0 with attribution, and was checked live on 2026-10-29: the dataset was updated 2026-09-30 and covers HU, `I56`, 2022. Conservative choices:

- **A benchmark is a reference, not a fact about the business.** It never becomes a `Field` on the profile, never feeds the diagnosis or a proposal, and never speaks as a finding. Whether it may is **I-44a** (decided 2026-11-03: a large, like-for-like margin gap may, see below).

  - **The API.** It is `…/statistics/1.0/data/{dataset}?format=JSON&lang=EN&{dim}={code}`, with a filter repeated for several codes. ## Idea 53: one-tap approvals on your phone (owner, 2026-10-29)
